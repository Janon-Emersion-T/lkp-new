Frequently asked

Before investing in SEO.

Clear answers about SEO costs, timelines, rankings, technical work, content and ongoing optimisation.

How much does SEO cost?

SEO pricing depends on the website, market, competition, technical condition, content requirements and scope of ongoing work. We assess the requirement before defining an appropriate engagement rather than applying one package to every business.

How long does SEO take to produce results?

There is no responsible universal timeframe. Technical fixes can affect performance relatively quickly, while competitive rankings, content authority and sustained organic growth usually require continued work over time.

Can you guarantee first position on Google?

No responsible SEO provider can guarantee a specific organic ranking. Search results are controlled by search engines and influenced by competition, algorithms, relevance, authority and many external factors. We focus on defensible improvements rather than ranking guarantees.

Do you provide technical SEO?

Yes. Technical SEO can include crawling and indexation analysis, redirects, canonicals, architecture, structured data, performance, mobile considerations and other implementation issues affecting organic search.

Do you create SEO content?

SEO engagements can include content strategy, commercial-page optimisation, supporting content planning and content development where required. Content should serve users first while clearly communicating relevance and expertise to search engines.

Can you work on an existing website?

Yes. We can audit an existing website and work within its current platform where practical. When technical limitations restrict meaningful improvement, we explain those constraints before recommending larger development changes.

Do you provide local SEO?

Yes. Local SEO can form part of the strategy for businesses targeting customers within specific cities, regions or service areas, depending on the business model and competitive landscape.

How do you measure SEO performance?

Measurement can include search visibility, impressions, clicks, rankings, organic traffic, landing-page performance and relevant conversions. The useful metrics depend on the commercial objective rather than rankings alone.

Can you provide SEO for international markets?

Yes. SEO strategy can be developed for international markets, including market-specific search research, site structure and content requirements appropriate to the agreed target markets.
