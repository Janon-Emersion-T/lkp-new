Frequently asked

Before we take responsibility for the infrastructure.

Straightforward answers about hosting, domains, migrations, maintenance and ongoing infrastructure management.

How much do hosting and maintenance services cost?

Pricing depends on the application, traffic, server resources, storage, backup requirements and level of ongoing management. We establish the infrastructure requirement before recommending an appropriate service and cost.

Can you manage a domain we already own?

Yes. Where the registrar and account access allow it, we can work with existing domains and manage the required DNS, nameserver, subdomain and service configuration without requiring a new domain.

Can you migrate our existing website or application?

Yes. We first review the existing environment, application, database, DNS and dependencies, then plan the migration around the technical requirements and acceptable service interruption.

Do you provide SSL certificates and HTTPS configuration?

Yes. SSL/TLS certificate installation and HTTPS configuration can form part of the hosting or infrastructure setup, depending on the environment and service requirements.

Are backups included?

Backup requirements are defined according to the service and maintenance arrangement. We consider what needs to be backed up, how frequently and how recovery would be handled rather than assuming every workload requires the same policy.

Can you maintain systems hosted with another provider?

Potentially, yes. We first assess the provider, available access, server environment and technical constraints. If the environment can be managed responsibly, ongoing maintenance can be scoped accordingly.

Do you support businesses outside Sri Lanka?

Yes. Domain, hosting, deployment and maintenance work can be managed remotely for businesses in Sri Lanka and international markets where the required infrastructure access is available.
