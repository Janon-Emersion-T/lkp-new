Frequently asked

Before we start.

A few straightforward answers to the questions businesses usually ask before commissioning a website.

How much does a business website cost?

Cost depends on the scope, functionality, content requirements, integrations and level of custom design. We can provide a defined package for straightforward requirements or prepare a custom quotation for more complex projects.

How long does website development take?

Delivery time varies with scope. A focused business website can move considerably faster than a custom platform with integrations and complex workflows. We establish the delivery plan before development begins.

Can LKProfessionals handle the domain and hosting?

Yes. Domain registration, hosting, deployment, SSL, maintenance and ongoing technical support can be managed as part of the engagement.

Will the website be SEO friendly?

We build with technical SEO foundations in mind, including semantic structure, metadata, responsive behaviour, performance and crawlability. Ongoing SEO campaigns are available separately where competitive organic growth is required.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We can assess the existing website, retain what remains valuable, address technical and UX limitations, and rebuild where necessary.

Do you work with businesses outside Sri Lanka?

Yes. LKProfessionals works with businesses locally and internationally, with projects delivered remotely through structured communication, review and deployment processes.
