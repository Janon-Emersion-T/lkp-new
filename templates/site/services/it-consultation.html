Frequently asked

Before we start.

Straightforward answers to the questions businesses usually ask before engaging an external technology consultant.

How much does IT consultation cost?

The cost depends on the scope of the engagement, the systems that need to be reviewed, the complexity of the decision and the level of analysis required. We first establish the consulting requirement and then define an appropriate scope rather than selling unnecessary consulting hours.

How long does an IT consultation take?

A focused advisory session or technical review may be completed quickly, while architecture assessments, transformation planning or complex system reviews require more investigation. The expected engagement period is defined once we understand the requirement.

Can you review our existing systems before recommending changes?

Yes. Understanding the current environment is often essential. We can review existing software, infrastructure, integrations, workflows and technical constraints before recommending what should be retained, improved, replaced or integrated.

Do you only recommend technologies that LKProfessionals develops?

No. Consultation should begin with the requirement rather than a predetermined product. Where an existing platform, third-party service or another implementation approach makes more sense, that should be considered as part of the decision.

Can you help us choose between building and buying software?

Yes. Build-versus-buy decisions should consider more than the initial price. We can assess functional fit, customisation, integrations, ownership, recurring costs, vendor dependency, maintainability and long-term operational requirements.

Can LKProfessionals implement the recommendations afterwards?

Yes, where the recommended work falls within our engineering and digital capabilities. Consultation and implementation can remain separate, however, so the advisory stage does not depend on automatically awarding LKProfessionals the implementation work.

Do you work with businesses outside Sri Lanka?

Yes. IT consultation can be delivered remotely through structured discovery, documentation, technical reviews and stakeholder meetings for businesses in Sri Lanka and international markets.
