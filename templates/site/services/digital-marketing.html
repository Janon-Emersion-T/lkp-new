Frequently asked

Before we start marketing your business.

Straightforward answers about strategy, content, social media, paid campaigns, budgets, measurement and ongoing marketing.

How much does digital marketing cost?

Cost depends on the channels, campaign scope, content requirements, advertising activity and level of ongoing management. We establish what the business actually needs before defining the appropriate marketing scope.

Which digital marketing channels should my business use?

That depends on your audience, offer, buying journey and objective. We do not recommend a channel simply because it is popular. The channel needs a defined role in reaching or converting the people your business needs.

Do you manage social media accounts?

Yes. Social media management can include planning, content, publishing and campaign coordination across the channels relevant to the agreed marketing strategy.

Do you run paid advertising campaigns?

Yes. Paid campaigns can form part of the engagement where appropriate. We consider targeting, creative, budget, campaign structure, conversion paths and measurement together rather than treating advertising as an isolated activity.

Is advertising spend included in your management fee?

Advertising spend and service fees should be treated separately unless a proposal explicitly states otherwise. This keeps media expenditure transparent and makes it easier to understand what is being paid to the advertising platform and what is being paid for strategy and management.

How quickly will digital marketing produce results?

There is no responsible universal timeframe. Paid campaigns can generate performance data relatively quickly, while brand growth, audience development and organic channels generally require more time. Results also depend on the offer, market, competition, budget and conversion experience.

How do you measure campaign performance?

Measurement depends on the campaign objective. Relevant indicators may include qualified traffic, enquiries, leads, conversions, acquisition efficiency and other meaningful actions. We avoid treating impressions, followers or engagement as business results when they do not reflect the actual objective.

Can you work with our existing website and marketing assets?

Yes. We can assess existing websites, landing pages, social accounts, creative assets and campaign history, then determine what can be retained, improved or replaced for the new strategy.

Can LKProfessionals handle the website or landing pages too?

Yes. Because LKProfessionals also provides web development, we can connect campaign requirements with landing pages, tracking and conversion improvements where the engagement requires them.

Can you manage digital marketing for international markets?

Yes. Digital marketing engagements can be planned and managed remotely for businesses targeting Sri Lankan or international audiences, subject to the channels, market requirements and agreed campaign scope.
