Frequently asked

Before we start.

Straightforward answers to the questions businesses usually ask before investing in custom software.

How much does custom software development cost?

Cost depends on the workflows, user roles, integrations, data model, interfaces, infrastructure and overall complexity of the requirement. We define the scope before providing a project quotation rather than forcing complex requirements into a generic package.

How long does custom software take to develop?

Delivery time depends on scope and complexity. A focused internal application may be delivered considerably faster than a multi-module platform with integrations and complex permissions. We establish milestones and delivery expectations before engineering begins.

Can the software integrate with our existing systems?

Where suitable APIs or integration methods are available, yes. We can connect payment providers, third-party platforms, existing databases, external services and other business systems as part of the solution architecture.

Who owns the custom software and source code?

Ownership, licensing, source-code access and any third-party dependencies should be defined clearly in the project agreement. We make those commercial and technical terms explicit before development begins.

What happens when our requirements change?

Business requirements evolve. We assess requested changes against the agreed scope, architecture, budget and delivery plan, then handle them as an approved adjustment or a subsequent development phase.

Can LKProfessionals maintain the software after launch?

Yes. Ongoing maintenance can include infrastructure, monitoring, issue resolution, security updates, operational support and further development as the system and business evolve.

Do you work with businesses outside Sri Lanka?

Yes. LKProfessionals can deliver software projects remotely through structured discovery, communication, review, testing and deployment processes for both local and international businesses.
